For phone photos taken anywhere: get a clean ID photo in one step.

  1. Open Gemini or ChatGPT and attach the new hire’s photo.
  2. Paste the prompt below and send it.
  3. Download the result and upload it here. The app removes the green background itself.
  • Check the face still looks like the person before uploading. If it doesn’t, ask again or use the original photo.
  • If the person wears green, change #00FF00 to solid blue #0000FF in the prompt.
  • A transparent PNG works too, if the tool makes a real one.
  • Employee photos are personal data. Use your company’s approved AI account.
